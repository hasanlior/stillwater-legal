PRIVACY POLICY

STILLWATER (iOS, iPadOS, Windows) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

STILLWATERSupportTerms of UseTürkçe

In short

STILLWATER does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it works offline.

What stays on your device

  • Your save game (progress, items, settings and control bindings) is written only to the game's own storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere.
  • If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include the game's storage in those backups under Apple's own terms.

Data we collect

None. The game makes no network requests of its own. The App Store's "Data Not Collected" label applies.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Purchases and downloads are handled by the App Store under Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example if an online feature is added), this page will be updated and the date above changed before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hasanlior/stillwater-legal/issues.
